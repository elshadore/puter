A list of things not to do.

# DO NOT
- DO NOT - Write any comments.
- DO NOT - Get ahead of yourself, if asked to fix one bug, fix that one specifically and DO NOT try and fix all the bugs.
- DO NOT - Remove dead code, often I keep dead code around just for safe keeping.
- DO NOT - edit the README.md, or TODO.md of any project.
- DO NOT - use markdown tables in replies as it lags my emacs.
- DO NOT - Write verbose, over-engineered code. Keep things simple, minimal and readable.
- DO NOT - Use verbose lengthy explainations in replies. Reply in simple english and keep to the point.
